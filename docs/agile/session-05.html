Session 5

Running the Sprint and Daily Scrum

Why it mattersLead prep: 45–60 minTeam session: 45–60 min
Why it matters: A useful standup helps the team adapt toward the Sprint Goal; an informal status round hides stalled work and dependencies.

Purpose of the Daily Scrum

The team inspects progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapts the plan for the next day. It is not a report to the lead. The board should make movement, aging work, blocked items, and work in progress visible.

Facilitation techniques

  • Start at the Sprint Goal, then walk the board from right to left: finish work before starting more.
  • Ask who needs to collaborate today and what threatens the goal.
  • Capture blockers and dependencies as visible follow-up work with an owner.
  • Park deep technical troubleshooting for immediately after the 15-minute meeting.

Common mistakes and coaching prompts

Weak patternCoach toward
“Yesterday I worked on code.”“Which item moved, and what is its next state?”
Everyone starts new work.“What can we finish before pulling another item?”
Ten-minute debugging debate.“Who needs to stay after standup to resolve this?”
Blocked item stays Active.“Mark the blocker and name the dependency owner.”

Learning objectives

  • Use Azure Boards during a focused 15-minute Daily Scrum.
  • Talk about movement, blockers, and the Sprint Goal rather than personal status.
  • Separate coordination from deep troubleshooting.

Weak vs useful update

Weak: “I am still working on the screen.” Useful: “The address validation story is in Development; the database mapping is ready for lead review, and I need the UAT owner to confirm the invalid-date case today.”

Exercise: board-driven standup

Display a mock board with one item in Development for four days, one blocked by a database dependency, and one ready for shelveset review. Walk the board from right to left. Identify the next collaboration, update the states, and park any solution design discussion.

Key rules / questions

  • What is the next state for this item?
  • What threatens the Sprint Goal?
  • Can someone help finish existing work?
  • Does this need a board update or a follow-up conversation?
