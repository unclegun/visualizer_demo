Razor Placement Guidance

  • Layout-level visuals: gradient dividers, sticky navbar state, and scroll progress bar belong in _Layout.cshtml.
  • Feature-level visuals: hero variants and dashboard panels fit shared partials under Views/Shared or Pages/Shared.
  • Page-level visuals: table wrappers, form shells, and comparison cards are best in page-scoped markup.
HTML + JS 
<div class="scroll-progress"><span data-scroll-progress></span></div>
<nav class="navbar site-navbar sticky-top">...</nav>

<section class="section-shell" data-reveal data-reveal-delay="80">
  <h2>Operations Summary</h2>
  <p>Subtle animation clarifies hierarchy without distracting users.</p>
</section>

Accessibility + performance defaults

All motion utilities use transform/opacity, include reduced-motion fallback, and avoid mandatory animation for core interaction paths.

When not to use premium effects

Avoid dense effects in high-frequency CRUD screens where speed and scannability matter more than visual atmosphere.
