Razor implementation notes

  • Render each form section as a partial for maintainable validation boundaries.
  • Keep summary sidebar in page markup and update progress from model state.
  • Use ModelState summary callouts near the form header.

When to avoid these patterns

  • Avoid sticky sidebars on narrow mobile workflows.
  • Reduce chip/toggle count where business rules are simple.
  • Do not hide critical options only behind advanced filter toggles.
