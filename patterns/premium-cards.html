Comparison Card (Emphasis)

Use emphasis only for one primary card per row to avoid visual competition.

Razor Pages placement

  • Shared KPI cards are ideal as reusable partials.
  • Executive rows fit dashboard index pages with server-driven metric models.
  • Expandable notes can be page-scoped where domain detail varies.

Accessibility and tradeoffs

  • Spotlight effect is purely decorative and should never carry meaning.
  • Expandable cards must expose clear button labels and aria-expanded state.
  • Avoid stacking too many animated metrics in data-dense rows.
Razor Partial 
@* Views/Shared/_MetricCard.cshtml *@
<article class="premium-card card-accent-top p-3" data-spotlight-card>
  <p class="text-subtle mb-1">@Model.Label</p>
  <p class="metric-value" data-counter-target="@Model.Value">0</p>
  <p class="metric-trend mb-0">@Model.TrendText</p>
</article>
